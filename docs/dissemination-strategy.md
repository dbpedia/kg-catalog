# DBpedia Knowledge Graph Catalog — Dissemination Strategy

## 1. Purpose

The DBpedia Knowledge Graph (KG) Catalog is an open, community-driven resource for discovering, accessing, and maintaining information about knowledge graphs. Catalog metadata is managed transparently in a public repository. The catalog is published through a lightweight workflow.

This strategy establishes a systematic and sustainable approach to increasing awareness of the catalog, encouraging contributions, supporting adoption, and building a community around it. It complements the catalog's development and release processes but does not replace technical documentation or individual campaign plans.

## 2. Objectives

1. **Increase awareness.** Make the catalog widely known and recognized among KG publishers and consumers, including researchers, developers, and organizations.
2. **Grow and maintain catalog coverage.** Encourage publishers and community members to register knowledge graphs and keep existing metadata up to date.
3. **Promote discovery and use.** Help potential users find relevant knowledge graphs and understand how to access their data.
4. **Strengthen community and build relationships.** Attract contributors, gather feedback, and build relationships with KG projects and relevant communities.
5. **Demonstrate sustained progress and trust.** Communicate catalog improvements, metadata releases, and transparent maintenance practices over time.

## 3. Target Audiences

The DBpedia Knowledge Graph Catalog dissemination strategy focuses on two primary target audiences: **Knowledge Graph Publishers** and **Knowledge Graph Consumers**.

| Target audience | Description | Main interests | Desired actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **KG Publishers** | Individuals, research groups, projects, and organizations that create, publish, or maintain knowledge graphs | Increasing the visibility, discoverability, accessibility, and reuse of their KGs | Register their KGs in the catalog, maintain accurate and up-to-date metadata, and promote their catalog entries |
| **KG Consumers** | Researchers, developers, data practitioners, and organizations that search for, access, and use knowledge graphs | Discovering relevant KGs, accessing available datasets and versions, and integrating KG data into research and applications | Explore the catalog, discover and download KGs, use them in research or applications, and provide feedback |

### Audience-Specific Messaging

Dissemination activities should communicate the benefits of the catalog to each audience:

- **KG Publishers:** Increase the visibility and discoverability of your knowledge graphs by registering them in an open, community-driven catalog.
- **KG Consumers:** Discover and access knowledge graphs through a central catalog that provides structured metadata and information about available datasets and distributions.

## 4. Core messages

Communication should consistently highlight the following characteristics and benefits of the DBpedia KG Catalog:

- **Open and community-driven:** Anyone can propose new entries or corrections.
- **Transparent:** Metadata and changes are publicly visible and reviewable.
- **Facilitates discovery and access:** Users can explore KGs and find information about their available distributions and access methods.
- **Continuously maintained:** Catalog metadata is updated and published through a regular release process.
- **Lightweight and sustainable:** The system relies on automated workflows rather than a dedicated application backend.
- **Increases KG visibility:** Publishers can make their KGs easier to discover and access by registering them in the catalog.

## 5. Dissemination channels

The following table summarizes the dissemination channels, prioritized according to their expected impact. Section 6 defines the specific activities, timing, and impact indicators for each channel.

| Channel | Primary purpose | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Targeted outreach** | Invite specific KG publishers, projects, and organizations to register, correct, or promote catalog entries. | High |
| **LinkedIn** | Maintain regular visibility through KG showcases, release highlights, and engagement with publishers and consumers. | High |
| **Mailing lists** | Reach relevant Semantic Web, Linked Data, and KG communities with substantial announcements and targeted calls for contributions. | High |
| **KG maintainer updates** | Provide registered KG maintainers with monthly updates on the status and usage of their KGs, using the contact information provided in the catalog metadata. | High |
| **Slack and other community platforms** | Interact directly with relevant communities, answer questions, and share contextual updates. | High |
| **DBpedia website/blog** | Publish official announcements and persistent, detailed content that can be referenced from other channels. | Medium |
| **Newsletters (DBpedia, GOBLIN)** | Inform the DBpedia and GOBLIN communities about catalog developments, promote KG discovery and reuse, and encourage contributions. | Medium |
| **Conferences and workshops** | Demonstrate the catalog, establish contacts with publishers and consumers, and collect feedback. | Medium |
| **GitHub repository and Releases** | Maintain the authoritative record of catalog updates, releases, documentation, and contribution opportunities. | Medium |


Priorities reflect the expected contribution of each channel to catalog growth, adoption, and community engagement:

- **High:** Channels expected to have the strongest direct impact on KG registrations, catalog usage, or sustained engagement.
- **Medium:** Channels supporting awareness, credibility, and adoption, with moderate or less direct impact.
- **Low:** Complementary channels with more limited reach or less predictable impact.

Priorities should be reviewed periodically based on measured results.

### 6.1. Targeted outreach

**Activities:**
- Identify important KGs not yet registered in the catalog.
- Contact KG publishers and invite them to register their KGs.
- Encourage publishers to promote and maintain their catalog entries.
- Track contacts, responses, and contributions.

**Timing:** Ongoing, with a monthly target of 1-5 publishers contacted.

**Impact measurement:** Publishers contacted, responses received, new KG registrations or metadata updates.

### 6.2. LinkedIn

**Channel:** DBpedia Association LinkedIn page.

**Activities:**
- Weekly *Knowledge Graph of the Week* posts.
- Monthly catalog release highlights.
- Announcements of major improvements and milestones (e.g. reaching 50 or 100 KGs).
- Tutorials, use cases, and community contributions.
- Encourage KG publishers to share relevant posts.

**Timing:** Weekly KG showcase; additional posts for releases and milestones.

**Impact measurement:** Impressions, engagement, shares, link clicks, catalog referrals.

- Invite communities to contribute KGs and provide feedback.
- Maintain a list of relevant mailing lists and their posting rules.

### 6.4. KG maintainer updates

**Channel:** Direct email to registered KG maintainers.

**Activities:**
- Provide KG-specific updates on metadata status and available versions.
- Share KG usage statistics when available and reliable.
- Invite maintainers to verify and update metadata.
- Encourage maintainers to promote their catalog entries.
- Verify maintainer contact details before sending updates, respect email preferences, and provide an option to opt out of recurring messages.

**Timing:** Monthly, following the catalog release.

**Impact measurement:** Maintainers reached, responses, metadata updates, continued maintainer engagement.

### 6.5. DBpedia website/blog

**Channel:** [DBpedia Blog](https://www.dbpedia.org/blog/)

**Activities:**
- Publish official announcements of major releases and milestones.
- Present new features, tutorials, use cases, and community achievements.
- Promote articles through other dissemination channels.

**Timing:** Major milestones; approximately quarterly when relevant.

**Impact measurement:** Article views, referrals, references, resulting contributions.

### 6.6. Newsletters (DBpedia, GOBLIN)

**Channels:** DBpedia Newsletter, GOBLIN Newsletter.

**Activities:**
- Publish concise catalog progress updates.
- Highlight new KGs, releases, and functionality.
- Promote KG discovery, reuse, and contributions.

**Timing:** According to newsletter publication schedules.

**Impact measurement:** Newsletter contributions, referrals, resulting engagement.

### 6.7. Conferences and workshops

**Potential events:** DBpedia workshops, GOBLIN events, LDK, SEMANTiCS, and other relevant conferences.

**Activities:**
- Present the catalog through talks, posters, and demonstrations.
- Demonstrate KG discovery, download, and registration.
- Establish contacts with KG publishers and collect feedback.

**Timing:** When relevant opportunities arise.

**Impact measurement:** Events attended, presentations delivered, contacts established, resulting contributions.

### 6.8. GitHub repository and Releases

**Channel:** [KG Catalog GitHub repository](https://github.com/dbpedia/kg-catalog)

**Activities:**
- Publish monthly catalog metadata releases and software releases as needed.
- Maintain release notes, documentation, and contribution instructions.
- Encourage contributions through GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
- Share relevant releases through other dissemination channels.

**Timing:** Monthly catalog releases; software releases and documentation updates as needed.

**Impact measurement:** Release regularity, external contributors, Issues and Pull Requests, KG registrations.

### 6.9. Slack and other community platforms

**Potential channels:** DBpedia Slack and relevant KG/Semantic Web communities.

**Activities:**
- Share significant announcements and updates.
- Participate in relevant discussions and answer questions.
- Invite feedback and KG contributions.

**Timing:** Aligned with major catalog releases, mailing list announcements, and milestones, or opportunistically when relevant discussions arise.

**Impact measurement:** Community interactions, feedback, contacts established, resulting contributions.

## 7. Impact evaluation

The KG Catalog team should evaluate dissemination effectiveness **quarterly**, using a small set of indicators that reflect both catalog growth and actual usage.

| KPI | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog growth** | Number of new KGs registered during the quarter |
| **Community contributions** | Number of external contributors and metadata updates |
| **Catalog usage** | Catalog visits, KG entry views, and download activity where reliable data is available |
| **Dissemination reach** | Engagement and referrals from communication channels, where measurable |
| **Publisher engagement** | Responses to outreach, maintainer feedback, and KG metadata reviews or updates |

At the end of each quarter:

1. Collect available metrics from catalog records, GitHub, website analytics, and communication channels.
2. Compare results with the previous quarter and record any gaps in measurement.
3. Identify activities that generated meaningful contributions or usage, not only impressions.
4. Review channel priorities and adjust activities and targets for the next quarter.

### Dissemination records

These records support the quarterly evaluation of dissemination activities described above.
